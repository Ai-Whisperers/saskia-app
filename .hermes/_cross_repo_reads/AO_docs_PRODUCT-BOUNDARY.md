## 2. Who may promise what

| Role | May promise | Must escalate to John |
|---|---|---|
| Sales agents | Anything in "In scope" column at list price | Custom scope, discounts, new product lines, SLAs, dates |
| Engineering | Feasibility opinions, estimates | Any external commitment |
| Research | Findings, briefs | Publication, media, external attribution |
| PO-support (management-coordinator) | Nothing externally. Maintains this registry + backlog hygiene only | Everything external |

## 3. Escalation & change control

- Boundary changes: PR to this doc + John's explicit approve (comment or session note).
- New products enter as **proposals** with audience/problem/deliverable/exclusions, priced by John.
- Quarterly: PO-support regenerates the "evidence of use" table (which products have active consumers) — no consumer → dormant, per operating rule #2.

## 4. What this doc is NOT

- Not a marketing site. Not a contract. Not pricing sheet final. It is the **boundary of ambiguity**: Sales cannot sell what is not listed; Engineering can reject out-of-scope work by citing a row.
