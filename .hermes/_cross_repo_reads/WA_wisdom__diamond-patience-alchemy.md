## ⚠️ COMMON PITFALLS

### **1. Premature Extraction**
- Pulling out before crystallization
- Result: Powdered graphite, not diamonds
- Solution: Trust the process, use indicators

### **2. Excessive Pressure**
- Crushing instead of crystallizing
- Result: Shattered potential
- Solution: Measure and adjust pressure

### **3. Impatience Switching**
- Abandoning one diamond for another
- Result: Multiple half-formed crystals
- Solution: Commit to completion

### **4. Wrong Environment**
- Temperature/pressure mismatch
- Result: Wrong crystal structure
- Solution: Match conditions to goal
