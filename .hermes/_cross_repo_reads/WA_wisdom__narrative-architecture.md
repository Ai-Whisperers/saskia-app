# 🏛️ NARRATIVE AS ARCHITECTURE

> **"The story builds the building."**

## 🔮 ORIGIN

From transcribed conversations about company identity, transformation, and the "Wizard Academy" metaphor. This wisdom recognizes that narrative is not just storytelling—it is the foundational architecture that shapes reality, drives transformation, and creates competitive advantage.

## 📖 DEFINITION

**Narrative as Architecture:** The principle that stories, identities, and meanings are the structural foundation upon which organizations, systems, and transformations are built. The narrative precedes and shapes the material.

### The Core Insight:
> "The story builds the building."

What you believe about your organization becomes its structure. What you tell clients about your capabilities becomes their expectation. What your team tells themselves about their mission becomes their motivation.

## 📊 NARRATIVE METRICS

### What to Measure
- **Clarity:** Can people articulate your story?
- **Consistency:** Does narrative match action?
- **Resonance:** Does it connect emotionally?
- **Spread:** Are others telling your story?
- **Impact:** Does it drive decisions?

### What Not to Measure
- Length of narrative
- Number of stories told
- Fancy presentations
- Others' timelines
